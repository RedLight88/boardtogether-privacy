BoardTogether Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

BoardTogether is a multiplayer board game for Android made by RedLight Software ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses, why, and who it is shared with.

Information the game uses

  • Player account ID. When you open the game, Unity Authentication gives you an anonymous player ID. If you sign in with Google Play Games, that ID is linked to your Play Games account so you keep the same account on other devices.
  • Google Play Games profile. If you sign in with Google, the game receives your Play Games player ID and display name. The display name is used as your default nickname.
  • Nickname and avatar look. Your nickname is shown to other players in the same room. Your avatar's color, eyes and hat are saved on your phone and shown to other players in the room.
  • Room information. When you create or join an online room, the room's name, code, player list and nicknames are stored by Unity Lobby while the room exists.
  • IP address and network data. Online games are relayed through Unity Relay, which needs your device's IP address to deliver game traffic. Local Wi-Fi games connect devices directly on your network.
  • Advertising data. The game may show ads. Ad providers may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about how you interact with ads, as described in their own privacy policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Purchases. If you buy an in-app item (for example, removing ads), the payment is handled by Google Play. We receive a confirmation of the purchase, not your payment details.

We do not ask for your real name, email address, contacts, photos, precise location or microphone. The game has no text or voice chat.

Why we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your account.
  • To let you create, find and join rooms and play with other people.
  • To show ads and process purchases.
  • To keep the game working and fix problems.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. It is processed by the services the game is built on:

  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Lobby, Relay, and ads if Unity Ads is used)
  • Google (Play Games Services, Google Play billing, and ads if Google AdMob is used)

Other players in your room can see your nickname and avatar.

How long it is kept

Room data is removed when the room closes. Your account ID is kept while you use the game. Your nickname and avatar settings stay on your phone until you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Deleting your data

To delete your BoardTogether account and the data linked to it, email us at the address below from your Google account or include your in-game nickname. We will delete it within 30 days.

Children

BoardTogether is meant for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you think a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
