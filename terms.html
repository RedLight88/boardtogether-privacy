BoardTogether Terms of Use

Last updated: 14 November 2026

BoardTogether is a multiplayer board game for Android made by RedLight Software ("we"). These terms are the rules for using the game. By playing in online rooms or Local Wi-Fi rooms you agree to them. The game asks you to agree before your first room.

Who can play

  • BoardTogether is meant for people aged 13 and over. If you are under 18, check with a parent or guardian before playing online.
  • Online rooms need a Google Play Games account. Guests can play on Local Wi-Fi.
  • Your account is yours. Don't share it, sell it or use someone else's.

Playing with other people

Rooms are shared with other players, sometimes people you don't know. Be kind. These are not allowed anywhere in the game, including chat messages, nicknames and room names:

  • Bullying, harassment, threats or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves or others.
  • Hate speech or insults based on race, ethnicity, religion, nationality, disability, sex, gender identity, sexual orientation or age.
  • Sexual content, or anything that sexualizes children.
  • Violent or shocking content, or content that promotes illegal activity, drugs or weapons.
  • Spam, advertising, scams, phishing or links meant to trick people.
  • Sharing anyone's personal information, such as their real name, address, phone number or photos, or asking people for theirs.
  • Pretending to be another player, a staff member or a company.
  • Cheating, using bugs to spoil games, flooding the chat or deliberately ruining a room for others.

Chat

  • Messages you send in a room are shown to everyone in that room, as a list and as a bubble over your head.
  • We don't store chat messages. They disappear when you leave the room.
  • Never share personal information in the chat.

Muting and reporting

  • Mute: open the chat, tap Players (or tap a name in the chat) and choose Mute. You stop seeing that player's messages and bubbles. Players signed in with Google stay muted in later rooms too. You can unmute them the same way.
  • Report: in the same place, choose Report. Your email app opens with a report to us that includes the player's nickname, player ID, their recent messages and the room code. Add anything else you saw and send it.
  • We read every report. If someone broke these rules we may block their account from online rooms, temporarily or for good, or delete it. Threats of violence and anything involving harm to children may be passed to the police.
  • If you are in danger, contact your local emergency services first.

Ads and purchases

  • A short ad may play before you create or join an online room. Local Wi-Fi games have no ads.
  • Remove ads is a one-time purchase made through Google Play. It turns off the ads for good on the Google account that paid for it, including on a new phone or after reinstalling the game.
  • Payments, receipts and refunds are handled by Google Play under Google Play's refund policies. If a purchase is refunded, the ads come back.

The game

  • We keep improving BoardTogether, so games and features can change. Online play depends on services run by Unity and Google and may sometimes be unavailable.
  • The game is provided as it is. We do our best to keep it working, but we can't promise it will always be available or free of bugs.
  • Nothing in these terms limits the rights you have under the consumer protection laws of your country.

Stopping

You can stop playing at any time and ask us to delete your account. We may block or delete accounts that break these terms.

Changes

If these terms change, we will update this page and the date at the top. For important changes, the game asks you to agree again.
