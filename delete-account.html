Delete your BoardTogether account

BoardTogether, by RedLight Software

How to ask for deletion

  1. Send an email to robert.gm644@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my BoardTogether account".
  2. Send it from the Google account you sign in to the game with, or include your in-game nickname so we can find your account.
  3. We confirm by email and delete the account within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your BoardTogether player account (Unity Authentication player ID) and its link to your Google Play Games account.
  • Any room data still stored under your account.

What is kept

  • Nothing is kept by us after deletion.
  • Your nickname and avatar settings are stored only on your phone. Clear the app's data or uninstall BoardTogether to remove them.
  • Records of in-app purchases are kept by Google Play under Google's own policies.
